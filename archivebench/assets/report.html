Method and limits
  • Encoding time includes decoding and disk I/O. Quality scoring, source hashing, previews and archive verification are excluded.
  • Projected sizes exclude audio; the archive runner compresses retained audio separately. Long clips, motion, noise and background activity can change these estimates.
  • Scores apply to the selected output format and model. Chroma conversion for scoring does not measure all loss of original 4:2:2 detail.
  • Quick runs use one timed encode. Thorough runs use the median of three; timings are measurements, not deterministic promises.
  • The recommendation applies to this sample and measured candidates. A finite search may miss better settings; scores do not establish preservation quality.
